# First-run interview

Use `superpowers:brainstorming` for the process and `AskUserQuestion` (one to four questions per call, options with a recommendation) until the answers below are settled. Skip what the repo already answers. Do not guess names or roles.

1. **Audiences:** for A, B and C: name/role, what they decide with these reports, what they have said they care about, anything they dislike (jargon, long decks, hype).
2. **Occasion:** presented live (length in minutes) or read asynchronously? Who presents? Cadence is on demand, since the last report.
3. **Shape:** slide count and write-up length per audience (defaults in `audiences.md`), language/tone, spelling convention, whether to include a "decisions needed" slide.
4. **Figures:** preferred plot style, any figure that must appear every time (e.g. per-detector cross-detector AP).
5. **Sharing:** artifacts stay private; will the user share links, export .pptx/PDF, or send files? Anything that must not leave the lab (unpublished results, data paths).
6. **Terms:** words to avoid or always define; funding/milestone language the PIs use.
7. **Backfill:** should report #1 summarise the whole project so far or start at a date?

Then write `reports/INSTRUCTIONS.md` (answers + the rules from `SKILL.md`), `reports/glossary.md` (seed from `audiences.md`), and `reports/index.md`.
